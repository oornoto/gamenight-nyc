Game Night NYC

Game Night NYC is a small group of friends and collectors who get together a few times a year, in DUMBO and Manhattan, to play, trade, and compare collections. No dues, no league, no pressure. Just a table, some cards, and good company.

What we play

  • Magic: The Gathering
  • Dungeons & Dragons
  • Board games
  • Trading and collection nights
